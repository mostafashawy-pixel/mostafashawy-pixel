# Hi, I'm Mostafa

Founder of **[Placed](https://www.tryplaced.com.au)**, based in Melbourne.

**Placed: see real furniture from any store, at true size, in a 3D copy of your own home.**

Buying furniture online is guesswork. Every store has its own site, and none shows whether a sofa fits your room next to a rug from another store. Placed lets you search real products across retailers, bring in your home by address lookup, floor plan upload or iPhone LiDAR scan, and place real, buyable pieces at true size in 3D.

### What I'm building
- **Web app:** cross-retailer furniture search and a 3D home studio with an AI layout assistant
- **iPhone app:** LiDAR room scan into a 3D copy of your space
- **Catalogue:** real retailer 3D models from Australian stores

Now in an early pilot with university students in Melbourne.

### Stack
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Three.js (React Three Fiber), Supabase, Vercel, Vercel AI SDK, SwiftUI
